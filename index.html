      La Viuda NegraBlack Widow Poker

      Ante 10 · up to 6 players · 5 cards each · swap with La Viuda (all five or one card), clockwise · fold, or call “Me hasta aquí” to force the final cycle · best poker hand takes the pot

      Waiting for players
      Pot 0
      La Viuda
      How to play
      • The host opens a table and waits for players to take seats, then presses Start game now. Playing alone? Start solo and face The House.
      • Everyone antes 10 chips into the pot and gets 5 hidden cards. Five more cards sit in the middle: La Viuda.
      • First turn: you may pass, swap a single card with a face-down La Viuda card (blind swap), or — if nobody has taken it yet — swap all 5 of your cards for La Viuda, which flips it face-up for everyone. If you pass or swap one card, the chance to take the whole widow moves on to the next player.
      • Once La Viuda is face-up: swap one of your cards with one La Viuda card, or pass. Fold at any turn to leave the hand and forfeit your ante.
      • On your turn you can call “Me hasta aquí”. Everyone else then gets one last turn; you can’t change your hand.
      • Then all hands are revealed and the best poker hand wins the pot (ties split it).
